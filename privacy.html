MFilm Print Count – Privacy notice

MFilm Print Count is an internal tool used by one organisation's staff to record daily print counts, printers and stock at its centers.

What MFilm keeps about you: your name, role, centers, mobile number and e-mail (if given), a scrambled form of your password (nobody can read it), the readings and stock you enter, your messages (stored encrypted) and a log of sign-ins and changes.

Why: only to run MFilm – daily print counts, printers, stock, sign-in, password recovery codes and team messages. It is not sold, shared with other companies or used for advertising.

Google account data: MFilm runs inside the organisation's own Google account (Google Sheets, Drive, Forms and Apps Script). It uses that account only to store MFilm data, send MFilm e-mails (sign-in and recovery codes, alerts) and update its own code. It does not read anyone's other Google data.

Who can see it: your admins, by role (supervisors see their own centers).

How long: while you work with MFilm; old years can be archived, and backups are kept 30 days.

Your rights: ask your admin to see, correct or remove your details, or with any question or complaint about your data.
